mezura themes

Tune each color with the picker or by typing: the field accepts hex values (with or without #) and the 16 named ANSI colors (cyan, bright-magenta, ...), exactly like mezura's own parser. The badges show the contrast of each color against the background (aim for ≥ 4.5) and the ΔE distance between colors that sit next to each other in the output (aim for ≥ 45, below 25 they blend together). Named colors are previewed with Campbell scheme approximations; in a real terminal they follow your own color scheme. The five lines below each playground are the language slots of a theme file: paste them into a .txt in the themes dir, or into the style block of a config. Press + to spawn a new random playground.
